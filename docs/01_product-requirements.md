# Product Requirements Document


## Problem

Many people hsve trouble keeping track of how much money the spend. Without an
easy way to record expenses, it can be difficult to manage a budget and understand where their money is going.

## Desired outcome
The goal is to create a simple Python program that allows users to record their expenses, organize them into categories,
and caculate their total spending. This will help users beter understand their spending habits.


## Scope
The Personal Expense Tracker will allow users to enter their daily expenses, choose categories such as food, gas, and bills, and view their total spending . THe program will be designed for anyone who wants a simple way to manage their expenses.
## Out of Scope

The program will not connect to bank accounts, process payments, or automatically track payments, or 
automatically track purchases. Users will need to enter their expenses manually. The first version will focus on basic expense tracking. 


## Requirements


### The product must

- allow users to enter an expense amount and category
- caculate and display the total amount spent.
- display a list of recorded expenses.

### The product should

- save expenses to a CSV file/
- allow users to view spending by category.
- handle invalid inputs without crashing.
### The product could

- allow users to set monthly spending limits.
- display spending su,,aries for each month.
- create charts showing spending habits.
